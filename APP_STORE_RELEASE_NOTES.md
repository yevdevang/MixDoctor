# App Store Connect Release Notes

## Version 1.1.0 (Build 7)

### What's New

**Genre-Aware Analysis**
- Select your track's genre from 9 options (Pop, Rock/Indie, Hip-Hop/R&B, EDM/Electronic, Jazz, Classical/Orchestral, Metal, Acapella, Live) for more accurate, genre-specific analysis
- Get scoring and recommendations tailored to your music style's standards and expectations
- Genre selection is now available during import and can be updated from the dashboard

**Enhanced Mix Stage Selection**
- Choose your mix stage: Master (Streaming), Master (CD-Loud), or Mix (Pre-Master)
- Get scoring and analysis appropriate for your track's production stage
- Improved handling when you explicitly select master stage - your tracks are now correctly recognized as professional masters

**Improved Scoring System**
- More consistent scoring across different genre selections
- Better detection and handling of unmixed/raw recordings
- Enhanced scoring accuracy for classical and orchestral tracks
- Scoring now properly accounts for your selected mix stage

**Smarter AI Analysis**
- Enhanced AI analysis with improved contradiction detection
- More consistent feedback that matches your track's score
- Better recommendations tailored to your specific mix stage and genre

**UI Improvements**
- Streamlined file deletion process with better visual feedback
- Enhanced dashboard with improved genre and mix stage display
- Updated scoring information display for clearer understanding
- Various bug fixes and performance improvements
